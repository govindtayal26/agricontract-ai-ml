🚜 SIMPLE WORKFLOW

How AgriContract Works

Simple digital workflows for agricultural producers and buyers.

For Farmers

1

Create an Account

Register as a farmer and create your digital agricultural profile.

2

List Your Produce

Add crop name, variety, quantity, price and an optional crop image.

3

Manage Buyer Offers

Review incoming offers and accept or reject them from your farmer dashboard.

For Buyers

1

Browse Agricultural Crops

Search and discover agricultural produce listed by farmers.

2

Send an Offer

Enter your proposed price and submit an offer directly to the farmer.

3

Manage Contracts

Track submitted offers and communicate with farmers.
